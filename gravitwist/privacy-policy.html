Gravitwist Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Gravitwist (Android package com.nazgot.gravitwist), a puzzle game published by NazgotDev ("we", "us"), and the choices available to you.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. Gravitwist has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your progress (levels, stars, daily challenge results) and your settings (sound, vibration, language) are stored only on your device. We never receive them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID;
  • approximate location, estimated from your IP address;
  • interactions with ads (for example, which ads are shown or tapped);
  • diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance data.

This data is sent encrypted and handled by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Some ads are optional rewarded videos (for example, extra moves): they play only when you choose to watch them.

Your choices and consent

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the first time you open the game, a consent form from Google asks whether you agree to personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy options in the game's Settings. If you don't consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).

Deleting your data

Your game data lives only on your device: use Reset progress in the game's Settings, or uninstall the game, to delete it. Data collected by Google for advertising is managed by Google; see the links above, or reset your advertising ID.

Children

Gravitwist is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling information in a new way, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the game.
